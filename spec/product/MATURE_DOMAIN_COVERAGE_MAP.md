# Tracera Mature Domain Coverage Map

**Status:** canonical semantic coverage denominator for mature-spec recovery  
**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Rule:** domains are discovered from owner intent, historical product behavior, source archaeology and accepted standards/competitor research. They are not a requirement-count generator.

The existing 45 `TRC-R-*` requirements are the **Spine Contract**. They are strong but intentionally insufficient to represent the whole mature product.

## A. Canonical product truth — SPINE DECOMPOSED

Identity, aliases, typed accepted intent, immutable baselines, proposals, scope deltas, historical queries.

Current source-backed requirements include stable identity, baseline immutability, typed intent, proposal non-mutation and history.

## B. Product structure and ontology — PARTIAL

Mature scope:
- product/system/component/ecosystem hierarchy;
- arbitrary bounded decomposition/collapse;
- capabilities/features/requirements/obligations/targets;
- pages/screens/components/elements;
- architecture/services/APIs/data/events;
- policies/processes/problems/risks/decisions;
- variants/options/configurations/effectivity;
- reusable assets/capabilities;
- typed relation vocabulary and constraints;
- ontology/version evolution.

Current spine covers product graph distinction but not this breadth.

## C. Multi-projection product graph — PARTIAL

Mature scope:
- traceability;
- page flow;
- component hierarchy;
- product/business/technical/UI/security/performance perspectives;
- dependency/hierarchy/impact/journey/mind-map/gallery projections;
- projection consistency;
- filters/pivots/equivalence;
- progressive/bounded loading with explicit incompleteness;
- layout persistence and view identity;
- projection-specific semantics without changing canonical truth.

Historical source establishes substantial intent; atomic mature obligations remain to decompose.

## D. Traceability and engineering evidence — SPINE DECOMPOSED / BREADTH PARTIAL

Typed nodes/edges, bidirectional traversal, search, impact, exact evidence, provenance, bounded traversal.

Additional mature breadth:
- code/research/process/policy/data/model assets;
- contracts/SBOM/OpenAPI/test/telemetry/release links;
- confidence and uncertainty on inferred links;
- stale/conflicting links;
- equivalence/derivation/satisfaction/conflict semantics;
- source authority and reconciliation.

## E. Observations, evidence applicability and invalidation — SPINE DECOMPOSED

Exact scope, provenance, append-only history, reuse/admission decisions, candidate/configuration binding, stale/suspect/invalid/historical semantics, dependency-driven invalidation, conservative uncertainty and continuation semantics.

## F. Assessment and dissatisfaction — PARTIAL

Current spine covers evidence freshness, missing coverage and contradictions.

Mature dissatisfaction includes:
- bugs/violations;
- missing capabilities;
- unmet targets;
- performance regressions;
- usability/accessibility gaps;
- security/compliance gaps;
- stale/inconsistent product structure;
- orphaned/unreachable assets;
- broken journeys;
- variant/configuration-specific dissatisfaction;
- later predictive/simulated-user findings with explicit uncertainty.

Findings must be explainable, attributable, scoped and never silently mutate accepted truth.

## G. Product change control — PARTIAL

Proposal, review/authority, baseline acceptance, impact, scope delta, invalidation, rollback/supersession, history and comparison.

Needs atomic decomposition for graph-edit/change workflows and human/machine approval surfaces.

## Q. Extensibility/integrations — UNDERDECOMPOSED

## R. Configuration/variants/effectivity — UNDERDECOMPOSED

A product obligation/evidence/finding may apply to only specific version, variant, platform, deployment, customer or configuration.

Mature contract requires explicit applicability/effectivity rather than global truth leakage.

## S. Collaboration and multi-actor authority — UNDERDECOMPOSED

Human/operator/agent roles, proposal/review/accept authority, concurrent edits, conflicts, attribution, subscriptions/notifications where justified, and machine-generated proposal boundaries.

## T. Progress, grading and recovery quality — SPINE DECOMPOSED / RECOVERY PARTIAL

Separate mature completeness, stage readiness, journey closure, implementation realization, evidence coverage/freshness, uncertainty, regression, external validation and recovery quality.

No unknown denominator receives a fabricated percentage.

## U. AgilePlus federation — SPINE DECOMPOSED / CONTRACT PARTIAL

External work references, work/product authority split, evidence handoff, candidate/evaluation identity, no Done→Satisfied shortcut, failure isolation and versioned federation contract.

## V. Registry/ecosystem projection — PARTIAL

PhenoRegistry projects repo-local identity/status/revision without becoming a competing detailed authority.

## Coverage closure rule

A mature-specification pass is semantically complete only when every domain above has one explicit disposition:

- **DECOMPOSED** — atomic obligations + journeys/oracles are represented;
- **N/A** — source-backed rationale explains why the domain does not apply;
- **DEFERRED-MATURE** — it is part of mature intent, represented now, but not required by earlier stage projections.

`DEFERRED-MATURE` is not omission. Its mature obligations still belong in the mature contract.

No domain may remain merely PARTIAL/UNDERDECOMPOSED at final specification acceptance.
