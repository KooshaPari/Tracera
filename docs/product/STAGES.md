# Tracera stage design — no accepted projection yet

The previous CVP/MVP/GA assignments were computed from feature array positions. Those assignments are invalidated. Named stages are not ready for grading until their concrete FR, dependency and journey bindings have been reviewed.

## Preserved product-stage intent

A viable Tracera form must offer a coherent, durable product journey through accepted intent, product graph, evidence binding, assessment, dissatisfaction and inspectable reasons. External resolution and re-verification must respect the boundary between work completion and product acceptance. Precisely which supported journey variants define each stage remains a source-backed specification decision, not a fixed percentage.

The mature contract is the overall intended product, not an implementation afterthought. Stage projections share canonical FR identities, may diverge where justified, and explicitly mark required, optional, excluded and not-applicable obligations. Dependencies may not be silently omitted to shrink a stage.

For each projection specify closed outcomes, necessary features, mandatory leaf requirements and NFRs, environment, acceptable temporary adapters, acceptance gates, evidence obligations and transition debt. Counts are calculated afterward. No stage gets 'the first N' records.

Report mature-horizon completeness separately from stage readiness, core/feature closure, real journey viability, structural shape and growth continuity. Empty or invalidated projections are ungradable, never achieved.
