# Proposed product specification deltas

Status: proposed reconciliation into existing requirements and owners, not a replacement specification authority. Crosswalk IDs refer to recovered capability families, not newly accepted repository requirement IDs.

## A. Persistent editable product graph

Stable typed entities, relationships, profile versions and applicable facts SHALL cover declared software/hardware/other profiles. UI/API/SBOM/source/test/requirement/documentation/spatial lenses SHALL resolve the same canonical identity and baseline. Unsupported domains, missing entities and incomplete coverage SHALL remain visible. Atomicity means the smallest behavior meaningful in the declared scope, not an unbounded universal decomposition.

A product-changing gesture SHALL create a proposal binding base revision, actor authority, affected entities, preconditions, expected outputs and reversible candidate. Deterministic adapters may compile exact diffs; unsupported changes become bounded human/agent requests through AgilePlus or a declared compatible executor. Worker completion, merge and work acceptance SHALL NOT independently satisfy product obligations.

Proposed oracle: software and hybrid fixtures survive import, two-view edits, restart, export and comparison with exact identity/revisions. Negative cases include incompatible types, stale revision, unauthorized actor, cycles, pagination/truncation, partial evidence, duplicate receipt and failed adapter. No such oracle has been executed here.

## B. Product-world scenario and simulation

A Scenario SHALL bind ProductBaseline, environment/configuration, actors, devices/peripherals, initial state, ordered/branching actions, model/solver versions, seed where relevant, assumptions and output metrics. A Run preserves immutable inputs and results independently of rendering. Scene geometry SHALL distinguish physical, measured, semantic and illustrative truth consistently with #1104/#1122.

Proposed fidelity levels (engineering safeguards, not owner-fixed labels): semantic playback; behavioral simulation with declared implementation correspondence; physical/ergonomic simulation with units, coordinate frames, geometry/pose uncertainty and solver validation; empirically calibrated prediction with measured comparator, applicability range and error distribution. Unknown dimensions, timing or motion remain unknown.

Desk, hands, peripherals, couch and device scenes are intended examples. A replayable simulation can predict friction or reach under assumptions; it cannot prove ergonomic safety, deployed behavior or acceptance. Compare predictions with measured real-world journeys, preserve disagreement and invalidate stale calibration after model changes. Product realization enters proposal/candidate/evaluation review. Deployment or physical actuation requires separate authority.

## C. Truthful typed views and rich cards

API lenses SHALL use typed product API entities: product ID, method/path or protocol operation, contract revision, implementation mapping and verification status. Requirements SHALL NOT inherit GET. Platform reference SHALL be separately labeled. Missing API models produce unavailable/not-modeled states. Contract-defined, route-mounted, deployed and independently verified are distinct milestones.

Each lens declares its question, entity/filter/query contract and interactions. API/UI/SBOM/story lenses must show meaningful product-specific semantics, not renamed identical rows. Rich cards may present source-bound media/interactive artifacts, structured details and recursive story→step/use-case→atomic decomposition. Breadcrumbs and cross-links preserve identity. Preview and navigation are separate accessible actions; absent media, loading, privacy and security states remain explicit. No fixed preview percentage or grid is mandated.

Metrics SHALL distinguish known zero, missing, error, stale and invalid denominator. Browser checks must exercise multiple charts, nested containers, resize/zoom and keyboard use; bounding-box assertions and screenshots supplement source tests. No speculative CSS repair is accepted by this document.

## D. Federation and research

Tracera owns persistent product applicability; AgilePlus owns execution/spec/work transitions. Preserve existing identifiers and accepted federation semantics. Envelopes bind baseline, accepted spec revision, assignment/attempt, exact candidate, evaluator configuration, criterion outcomes and evidence. Idempotent replay cannot duplicate changes; mismatched baselines become stale/pending review. Do not require Tracera for standalone AgilePlus or duplicate its permanent work authority.

External papers/repositories/community ingestion SHALL preserve source versions, extraction locations, licenses and applicability. Hypotheses, forecasts and measured results are separate. Experiments bind current configuration, costs, uncertainty, kill gates and reproducible evidence. Forum signals cannot become universal facts. Self-hosting, developer-tool integration, machine-readable edits, CLI/API/MCP/user interfaces and wireframe/code deep links remain recovered early obligations; their missing detailed matrix rows are not reconstructed here.
