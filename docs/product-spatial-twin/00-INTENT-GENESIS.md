## Inferred design intent — assistant proposal, not user command

The goal may be realized through semantic assemblies, exploded/cutaway models, flow-machine and topology views, optional literal CAD, and synchronized 2D/list views. Geometric metaphor must preserve canonical IDs and must not invent physical components, execution authority or evidence.

## Genesis problem statement

Existing software city and 3D node-link visualizations do not intrinsically explain capability-to-implementation-to-runtime-to-verification relationships. Raw density becomes unreadable. A semantic physicalization could let observers disassemble a functional product, follow a failure, and distinguish proposed from implemented or verified behavior. That benefit is a hypothesis requiring comparative tasks, not a proven claim.

## Explicit exclusions

- Not a new Tracera product/registry; not a repository-file city as the canonical ontology.
- Not a license to depict software metaphor as physical truth or represent missing evidence as health.
- Not a mandate to replace all 2D graph, matrix, source or accessibility representations.
- Not permission to start native agent orchestration, change fleet authority, or merge acceptance gates.
