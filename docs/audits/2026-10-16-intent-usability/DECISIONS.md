The latest finish request preserves all stubs and full page truth, while the first useful MVP is intentionally narrower. This is an explicit milestone distinction, not silent sunset authority. No repository deletion, release, migration or upstream publication follows from this plan. Broader restructuring requires an actual owner decision and consumer impact evidence.

Feedback questions for the pilot: Can the operator explain current behavior without reading the whole codebase? Is the detected gap genuine and scoped to the selected baseline? Does reconciliation remain cheaper after a source change? Can another agent resume with bounded context? Does the product beat a realistic manual/search/catalog approach after infrastructure and maintenance cost? Record negative answers and interventions.

If the advantage hypothesis fails, acceptable outcomes include an adapter, a narrower internal model or ending further expansion while preserving data/export paths. The audit does not prescribe sunset today. Re-estimate and decide from the first qualified journey rather than a scaffold count or stale roadmap.
